# AI contribution policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. You're responsible for what you
submit, whether it's your first contribution or you're a maintainer.

Read the whole patch or message before you submit it. Test code changes and
check technical claims against the source, documentation or behavior you've
observed. If a reviewer asks why you chose an approach or how it affects
users, you should be able to answer without the tool.

You can report a bug before you know its cause. If an entry is missing from a
replica, tell us where you added it, which replica you searched and what the
search returned. Those details save the reviewer a round of questions before
they can start investigating.

Keep bind passwords, production directory entries and unpublished security
reports out of external AI services. Use fictitious entries if a tool
needs an LDIF example.

## Disclosing AI use

If a significant part of your submission comes unchanged from an AI tool,
you must disclose it. For other uses, you should mention the assistance
when it would help a reviewer, for example if a tool suggested a diagnosis
you later verified. Routine help with spelling, grammar or clarity needs no
disclosure. This follows [Fedora's AI-Assisted Contributions Policy](https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/council/policy/ai-contribution-policy/).

Put the disclosure in the issue, pull request or comment. One sentence
naming the tool and what it did is enough. For example, "I used Copilot to
draft the regression test." Leave AI attribution out of commit trailers.

## Submissions and reviews

Submit the work yourself. Unattended AI agents may not open issues or pull
requests, post reviews or reply on your behalf. Answer questions in your own
words and keep revisions focused on the feedback.

A human reviewer decides whether to accept a contribution. Writing style
and AI-detector scores are unreliable indicators of authorship. If you have
a concern, point to something the contributor can check or fix. See the
[review guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md#reviewing-contributions).
